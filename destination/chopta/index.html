About Chopta & Travel Highlights

Known as the “Mini Switzerland of Uttarakhand”, Chopta is an unspoiled alpine meadow nestled inside the Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary. It serves as the base for the sacred Tungnath temple and Chandrashila 360-degree Himalayan summit.

💡 Insider Travel Tips from Local Guides

  • Chopta is an eco-sensitive zone; electricity is solar-powered in camps, carry power banks.
  • Start the Chandrashila trek before dawn (4:00 AM) to catch the golden Himalayan sunrise.
  • Rent trekking poles and crampons in Chopta during winter snow season.
  • Strictly "Leave No Trace" — carry your plastic waste back to Rishikesh/Haridwar.
